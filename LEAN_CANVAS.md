# Bhoomika Dental Clinic — Lean Canvas

## Problem

Patients in and around Badami often need dental care but are unsure which treatment to request, what a visit will feel like, and whether they can reach a trusted local clinician quickly. Existing clinic pages tend to provide a phone number without a clear next step, visual proof of the clinic, or an accessible way to ask questions before booking.

## Customer Segments

The primary segment is local families in Badami looking for check-ups, pain relief, preventive care, restorations, braces, or imaging. Early adopters are people searching on mobile after a symptom appears, parents arranging care for children, and patients who prefer Kannada or want a quick callback instead of a long form.

## Unique Value Proposition

**A calm, local dental visit made easier: see the real clinic, understand your options, and request the right next step in minutes.**

## Solution

The website combines clinic-owned photo and video proof, a specialty-led care grid that pre-fills the appointment flow, bilingual content, a callback path for people who are unsure, and a voice assistant that answers basic clinic questions without diagnosing.

## Channels

The primary channels are Google Search and Maps, WhatsApp or direct sharing, the clinic’s public GitHub-backed web presence, referrals, and the emergency phone number displayed in the utility bar.

## Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from in-clinic consultations and treatment plans across preventive care, imaging, restorative dentistry, orthodontics, endodontics, and oral surgery. The free Monday consultation is a local acquisition offer that should convert into appropriate follow-up care without inventing prices online.

## Cost Structure

Core costs are clinician time, clinic operations, consumables, imaging and equipment maintenance, phone follow-up, hosting, domain and media delivery, and the time required to respond to website leads. The key variable cost is staff time per qualified request and missed-call recovery.

## Key Metrics

Track appointment-form completion rate, specialty-card-to-form conversion, callback submission rate, percentage of submissions with complete phone and care details, response time to new leads, Monday consultation attendance, and treatment-plan follow-up rate. The North Star metric is **completed, appropriate clinic visits originating from a clear website request**.

## Unfair Advantage

The defensible advantage is the combination of a real local clinic identity, clinic-owned media, clinician-led explanations, Kannada accessibility, fast human callback, and a simple care-navigation flow that reduces uncertainty before a patient visits.

## Riskiest Assumptions to Validate

Patients will trust clinic-owned photos more than generic stock imagery; specialty selection will improve form completion; Kannada and voice support will increase qualified enquiries; and a callback option will recover patients who do not yet know which treatment they need.
