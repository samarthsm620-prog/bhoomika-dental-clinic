Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Bhoomika Dental Clinic uses the information you choose to share to respond to appointment and callback requests. We aim to collect only what is needed to coordinate care.

Information you provide

This may include your name, phone number, email address, preferred visit details, care area, language, and the concern you describe. Do not send emergency information or highly sensitive medical records through the public form. Files selected in the form are not uploaded.

How we use it

Clinic staff may use submitted details to contact you and coordinate a requested visit. Appointment notification emails omit the selected care area and free-text dental concern. An appointment request is not confirmed until the clinic contacts you.

Booking records

Appointment and screening submissions may be stored in a private Google Sheet managed by Bhoomika Dental Clinic for intake, follow-up, duplicate-request prevention, and operational record keeping. Access is limited to authorized clinic staff and the sheet is not published publicly.

Email and payment

When configured, the appointment service submits clinic notifications and a patient receipt request through EmailJS. An EmailJS acceptance response does not prove delivery to an inbox; if you do not receive an email, call the clinic at +91 9481731482. PhonePe QR payments are verified manually by clinic staff. Payment or submitting a request does not itself confirm an appointment. The form does not collect card details or offer SMS notifications.

Analytics and cookies

Optional Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Accept analytics” in the privacy notice and only when the clinic has configured a valid property. It may receive basic page-view, browser, and device information. The site does not intentionally send appointment fields, names, contact details, symptoms, or dental concerns as analytics events or parameters. Essential browser storage remembers your choice; use “Cookie settings” in the homepage footer to change it, or clear the saved choice in your browser settings. The clinic should review its Google Analytics configuration and legal obligations before enabling tracking.

Third-party services

The site may use Cloudflare Pages, EmailJS, Google Calendar link generation, Google services, and Manus automation for the separate callback flow. If you choose “Continue in WhatsApp,” the details in the form (including any concern you entered) are placed into a WhatsApp message addressed to the clinic. Appointment requests do not use a payment webhook. Third-party processing is governed by each provider’s own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

You may contact the clinic to ask about a request you submitted or ask that the clinic stop contacting you. For privacy questions, call +91 9481731482.

Medical safety

This website provides general information and intake support, not diagnosis or emergency care. For severe facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, a knocked-out tooth, or difficulty breathing or swallowing, seek urgent local medical care.
