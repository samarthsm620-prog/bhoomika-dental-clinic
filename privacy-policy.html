Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Bhoomika Dental Clinic uses the information you choose to share to respond to appointment and callback requests. We aim to collect only what is needed to coordinate care.

Information you provide

This may include your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment details, care area, language, and the concern you describe. Do not send emergency information or highly sensitive medical records through the public form.

How we use it

Clinic staff may use submitted details to contact you, coordinate a requested visit, answer a callback request, and maintain the clinic’s operational records. An appointment request is not a confirmed appointment until the clinic contacts you.

Email and communications

When configured, the appointment service sends a clinic notification and a receipt to the email address you enter. The website does not ask for, collect, or need your email password. Phone follow-up is handled by the clinic team.

Analytics and cookies

Optional analytics will load only after you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie notice and only when the clinic has configured a valid analytics property. Essential browser storage may remember your consent choice. You can clear this choice through your browser settings.

Third-party services

The site may use Cloudflare Pages, EmailJS, Google services, Manus automation webhooks, and Google Workspace tools for hosting or clinic operations. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

You may contact the clinic to ask about a request you submitted or ask that the clinic stop contacting you. For privacy questions, call +91 9481731482.

Medical safety

This website provides general information and intake support, not diagnosis or emergency care. For severe facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, a knocked-out tooth, or difficulty breathing or swallowing, seek urgent local medical care.
